The League

The Juiced Open is a weekly iRacing league built for drivers who want to race — not time trial, not practice farm, not qualify for 45 minutes. You grid up and you race.

Every Wednesday and Saturday at 9:30 PM EST, races go live across four disciplines — Oval, Dirt Oval, Road/GT, and Dirt Road.

How Points Work

NASCAR-style ladder. P1 scores 55 points, P2 scores 54, P3 scores 53, and so on down to a minimum of 1. Your best result per week counts toward standings — so if you run all five races and win one, that win is what goes in the books.

Saturday runs three races, Wednesday runs two. Five races every week you can enter — you don't have to be perfect, you just have to show up and find your best result in something.

Miss a week entirely? The catch-up rule has you covered. Your next eligible results automatically backfill your missing slots. Life happens — one bad week doesn't knock you out of contention.

The Stream

This isn't someone screen-recording iRacing. This is a production — and one specific thing makes it different.

The Crash Cam
A dedicated second iRacing account runs on track as a spotter — no HUD, no car, just a free camera that follows the action. It chases the incidents, the door slams, the last-lap moves. If something happens, the crash cam is already there. When you race in the Juiced Open, you're on stream.
The Rig
Billy runs a full motion simulator. When the car hits the wall, he feels it. Chat can trigger effects mid-race through Twitch bits — including boosting the motion 100%. Race night is live, loud, and genuinely unpredictable.
Moose
Chat can trigger an automated treat dispenser that feeds Moose, Billy's dog, mid-race. Yes, really. Someone will throw bits during a late-race battle and Moose will get a snack. This is the stream.
